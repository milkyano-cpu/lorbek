Australia's European prestige specialists

For over 40 years LORBEK Luxury Cars has traded in the world of luxury, sports and supercars, exotic and classic cars — from Ferrari, Lamborghini and Porsche to Rolls-Royce, Bentley, Aston Martin and Mercedes-AMG.

Two showrooms, in Port Melbourne and Sydney's Rushcutters Bay, hold a curated collection of European prestige cars, luxury SUVs and collectable classics. Every one is selected and presented by people who have spent their lives around them.
